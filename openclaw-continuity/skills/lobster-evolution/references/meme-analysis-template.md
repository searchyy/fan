Use this as the default answer shape when the target is a meme token.

## Use notes

- Put KOL framing, next buyers, and break conditions before contract details.
- Keep contract/security as the risk floor unless it can directly break the narrative.
- If the meme is mostly media搬运 and not real community discussion, downgrade the go-grade.
- If the concept is not tightly bound to this CA, explicitly warn that “concept survives, your bag may not.”
- Always explain *who buys next* and *what kills the story*.

## Go-grade calibration

- **只适合观察**: narrative weak, CA binding weak, or risk floor too dirty
- **可看**: narrative exists but next-buyer clarity weak or break risk near
- **可小仓**: narrative strong enough for tactical participation, but not clean enough to chase hard
- **可追**: rare; requires strong KOL framing, clear next buyers, expanding heat, and no immediate narrative-kill risk
