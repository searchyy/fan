# Meme Analysis Protocol

Use this protocol for meme tokens and narrative-driven tickers where price is mostly a function of attention, KOL framing, and rotating liquidity.

## Priority order

Analyze in this order, not in generic project-analysis order:

1. **KOL framing**
2. **Next-bagholder profile**
3. **Narrative break conditions**
4. **Narrative source and authenticity**
5. **Heat ↔ market-cap fit**
6. **Market-maker / operator intent**
7. **Media搬运 vs real community heat**
8. **Same-lane alternatives**
9. **Contract and chain risk floor**
10. **Go-grade**

The point is to understand who is manufacturing the meme, who is buying it next, and what event kills the story.

## 1) KOL framing (highest weight)

Identify the accounts that define the meme.

Questions:
- Who first called it?
- Who turned it from “coin” into “story”?
- Which phrases became the market’s anchor?
- Are the amplifiers original callers, aligned repeaters, media relays, or bots?

Extract:
- core callers
- relay accounts
- repeated phrases / slogans
- timing of framing waves

## 2) Next-bagholder profile (make this explicit)

Always state who the next buyers are likely to be.

Typical buckets:
- Chinese BSC momentum chasers
- KOL-follower retail
- symbol believers / identity buyers
- breakout confirmation chasers
- late FOMO tourists

For each bucket, explain:
- what makes them buy
- what price / market-cap zone attracts them
- what headline or phrase activates them

## 3) Narrative break conditions (move forward)

Do not wait until the conclusion section. Define break conditions early.

Check:
- KOL silence / defection
- key slogan disappearing
- market-cap anchor failing
- no expansion into new audience layers
- concept detaching from this CA and migrating to substitutes
- contract risk becoming common knowledge

Classify break into:
- soft break
- medium break
- hard break

## 4) Narrative source and authenticity

Determine whether the meme is:
- organic
- borrowed
- forced by KOLs
- media-amplified
- symbol-parasitic

Core question:
- Is the market buying the coin, the concept, or the phrase?

## 5) Heat ↔ market-cap fit

Judge whether current market cap is justified by current heat structure.

Look at:
- spread of discussion across account tiers
- number of unique talking points vs repeated copywriting
- whether heat is expanding or just echoing inside one clique
- whether market-cap growth outruns audience expansion

When mismatch appears, find the reason:
- thin float
- concentrated market making
- KOL convoy effect
- media relay inflation
- fake activity / bot echoes

## 6) Market-maker / operator intent

Add a dedicated “做局痕迹” layer.

Look for:
- suspiciously clean market-cap targets
- repeated slogan timing around breakouts
- coordinated timeline among callers
- low-float valuation jumps
- obvious belief engineering (“this is the Chinese leader”, “space is open”, etc.)

Do not claim manipulation without evidence; frame as intent/risk indicators.

## 7) Media搬运 vs real community heat

Separate:
- original discussion
- KOL amplification
- media summaries / alert bots / terminal screenshots
- bot or low-signal reposts

The meme is stronger when original discussion rises faster than relay content.
The meme is weaker when most “heat” comes from monitors, bots, and summary media.

## 8) Same-lane alternatives

Always compare against substitute tickers in the same lane:
- same concept
- same chain
- same audience
- same slogan family

Question:
- If buyers want this story, could they buy another ticker instead?

If yes, the meme’s moat is weak.

## 9) Contract and chain risk floor

Do not ignore contract risk, but downgrade it from center stage.

For meme analysis, contract review is the floor, not the story.

Only elevate contract risk to the headline if it can directly kill narrative credibility:
- transfer restriction
- owner-controlled flow
- blacklist / freeze / tax trap
- LP extraction risk

## 10) Go-grade

End with a simple action grade:
- **只适合观察**
- **可看**
- **可小仓**
- **可追**

This grade must reflect:
- narrative strength
- next-buyer clarity
- break-risk proximity
- contract risk floor

## Output spine

For meme tokens, prefer this output order:

1. one-line verdict
2. KOL framing
3. next-bagholder profile
4. break conditions
5. whether heat truly maps to this CA
6. heat vs market-cap fit
7. market-maker / operator intent
8. media relay vs real community
9. alternatives
10. contract risk floor
11. go-grade

Use the fixed answer skeleton in `meme-analysis-template.md` by default.

## 输出前自检（必须，不能跳过）

完成各版块分析后，输出前过一遍：

1. **10个版块有没有全做？** 缺哪个说为什么跳过
2. **每个版块有没有具体证据？** 不能只有定性结论
3. **KOL 定调版块** — 有没有点名具体账号和具体推文？
4. **接盘人画像** — 有没有说清楚是哪类人？什么价格激活他们？
5. **叙事破条件** — 有没有具体量化？（不是"可能破"，而是"当X发生时破"）
6. **可冲等级** — 是否和前面的分析自洽？（不能前面说叙事弱，后面给"可追"）
7. **数据来源** — 用的是实时数据还是推断？标注清楚

## Compression rule

After a meme review, save only reusable heuristics, such as:
- a new break trigger pattern
- a new KOL-framing archetype
- a better next-bagholder taxonomy
- a stronger heat-vs-market-cap mismatch rule
